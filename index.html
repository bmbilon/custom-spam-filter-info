Custom Spam Filter

Custom Spam Filter connects a Thunderbird sender-block action to Gmail account rules. When its owner blocks a sender, the local helper saves the rule in Gmail and verifies that it was retained.

Future matching mail skips the Inbox and is kept under the Blocked senders label. Gmail applies the rule independently of Thunderbird, including when the desktop app is closed.

The Google connection requests permission to manage Gmail settings, filters and labels, and to verify the selected account's identity. It does not request permission to read email bodies or send messages.

This tool is for its owner's private use. It is not offered as a public service.
