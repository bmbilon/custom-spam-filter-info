Custom Spam Filter

Use and controls

Effective October 30, 2026

Custom Spam Filter is provided for private use by its owner, Brett Bilon. It is not a public email-processing service.

What a block does

A sender block saves an account-level Gmail filter. Future matches skip the Inbox and are kept under the Blocked senders label. Messages remain recoverable; this rule does not permanently delete them. The selected message and older matching Inbox messages may be moved to Junk through Thunderbird's existing cleanup action.

Configured approved-correspondent exceptions are included in each server rule. Shared email providers and selected shared platforms are blocked by exact sender address; other senders are blocked by their full sending domain. The owner should review the scope of any block that affects an organization they correspond with.

Managing access and rules

The owner can inspect or delete rules in Gmail's Filters and Blocked Addresses settings and can revoke the helper's Google authorization at any time. Deleting a local Thunderbird rule does not remove the corresponding Gmail rule. Revoking authorization prevents further changes by the helper and does not remove rules already saved in Gmail.
